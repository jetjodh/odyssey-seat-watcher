FASTEST GITHUB-WEB SETUP

1. Fork https://github.com/divkhare/amc-good-seats
2. In your fork, open amc-node.js and click the pencil icon.
3. Replace its contents with the amc-node.js from this folder and commit.
4. Open .github/workflows/check-seats.yml and click the pencil icon.
5. Replace its contents with the check-seats.yml from this folder and commit.
6. In ntfy, subscribe to a long random topic name.
7. In GitHub, go to Settings > Secrets and variables > Actions.
8. Add a repository secret named NTFY_TOPIC with that topic name as its value.
9. Go to Actions > Check Odyssey Seats > Run workflow.
10. Turn on "Send a test notification only" and run it.
11. Then run "Scan and show matches in the log without notifying" once.
12. Finally, run normally with both switches off and leave the schedule enabled.

The watcher is already configured for one regular central seat in rows J-M
and showtimes from 11:00 AM through 8:30 PM.
